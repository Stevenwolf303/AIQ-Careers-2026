Our Story

Crafted with intention.

Volta began with a simple conviction: exceptional coffee deserves an exceptional journey. From the high-altitude farms where our beans are carefully hand-picked to the precise moment they reach your cup, every step is guided by craft and respect for the process.

We partner directly with growers who share our commitment to sustainable practices and fair compensation. Each lot is roasted in small batches at our studio to honor the distinct character of its origin.
