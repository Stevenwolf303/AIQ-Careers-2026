The Situation

Clearpath had spent years building a strong reputation through referrals and word-of-mouth, but their website told a different story. It looked like a template from another era — no clear service breakdown, no trust signals, and no way for prospects to request a consultation online. In a field where credibility is everything, first impressions were undermining their expertise.

The Objective
How do you make a small accounting firm feel as credible and modern as the larger competitors — while keeping it approachable?
The Approach

We focused on what makes Clearpath different — personal service, deep expertise, and long-standing client relationships — then built a brand and site that communicated those qualities immediately.

  • Redesigned the brand with a clean, professional visual system that balances trust with approachability
  • Structured the site around clear service pages, team credibility, and social proof
  • Built a streamlined lead capture flow with consultation request forms on every key page
  • Designed a responsive layout that reads well on both desktop and mobile
  • Created a content framework for case studies and client testimonials
