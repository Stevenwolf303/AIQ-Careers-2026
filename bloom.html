The Situation

Bloom Botanicals had been a neighborhood favorite for over a decade, known for stunning arrangements and personal service. But their brand still looked like it was designed in 2010 — a dated logo, no cohesive color system, and a website that didn't support online orders. They were losing ground to competitors with stronger digital presence, despite having a more loyal customer base.

The Objective
How do you take a beloved local shop and give it a brand that works in-store, online, and on social — without losing its soul?
The Approach

We immersed ourselves in the Bloom experience — visiting the shop, talking to regulars, and studying what made it special. Then we built a brand system that captured that warmth digitally.

  • Developed a botanical-inspired visual identity with a refined color palette and modern wordmark
  • Designed a clean, product-forward website with integrated online ordering
  • Created a flexible brand system that works across signage, packaging, and social media
  • Built an SEO-optimized site structure to capture local search traffic
  • Streamlined the customer journey from discovery to purchase
