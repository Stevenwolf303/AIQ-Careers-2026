About BrightPath

Trusted by families for over 15 years

We believe every child deserves a place where they feel safe, valued, and free to explore.

Since 2011, BrightPath Daycare has been a second home for hundreds of families in our community. Our approach blends structured learning with free play, allowing children to develop at their own pace while building the skills they need for school and life. Every classroom is led by certified early childhood educators who are passionate about making a difference in young lives.
