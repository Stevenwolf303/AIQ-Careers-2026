The Situation

Volta had built a loyal local following through farmers markets and a small cafe, but their online presence didn't reflect the quality of their product. The website was a basic template with no real brand voice, poor product photography integration, and a clunky checkout that was costing them sales. They needed a digital experience that matched the craft behind every bag.

The Objective
How do you translate the sensory experience of a specialty roaster into a digital storefront that drives direct sales?
The Approach

We spent time understanding Volta's sourcing story, roasting process, and what made their customers loyal. Then we designed a brand and e-commerce experience that brought that story to life online.

  • Refined the brand identity with warmer tones, tactile textures, and premium typography
  • Designed a product-led e-commerce experience centered around storytelling and origin details
  • Built a streamlined checkout flow optimized for subscription and one-time purchases
  • Created rich product pages that connect each roast to its origin and tasting notes
  • Implemented a responsive design that converts on both desktop and mobile
