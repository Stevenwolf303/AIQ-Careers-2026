Brightpath Daycare

Repositioning a legacy daycare brand for modern families.

Industry Childcare
Services Brand Identity, Website Design, Development
Year 2026
Homepage mockup
The Situation

Brightpath had been serving families in their community for over 15 years, but their brand hadn't kept up. The website felt dated, the messaging was unclear, and the overall presence didn't reflect the warmth and quality parents experienced in person. In a market where trust is everything, first impressions were costing them enrollments.

The Objective
How do we make a daycare feel instantly trustworthy, warm, and modern online?
The Approach

We worked closely with the Brightpath team to understand what made their care special, then built a brand and website that communicated it clearly.

  • Redesigned the visual identity with a warmer, more approachable palette and modern typography
  • Simplified the messaging to lead with trust, safety, and community
  • Restructured the site around what parents actually need: enrollment, programs, and contact
  • Designed a responsive site that feels welcoming on any device
  • Built clear conversion paths from first visit to enrollment inquiry

The Result

The new brand and website gave Brightpath a digital presence that matched the quality of their care. Parents could now find the information they needed quickly, and the overall experience felt trustworthy from the first click.
