Our Opportunity

A $3.5 trillion asset class, run manually in spreadsheets.

Multifamily operators manage billions in assets. They set rents, run marketing campaigns, and make investment decisions that affect how millions of people live.

Most of this happens in spreadsheets. Pricing is manual. Marketing is manual. And the data needed to do any of it well has never really existed.

We're building the data infrastructure and AI systems that change that.

ApartmentIQ is our data and decisioning platform:

Market Surveys

Show how operators are performing against competitors.

Market Research (Explore Pro)

Helps operators decide where to buy, build, and redevelop.

Revenue Management (Daylight)

Recommends the right rents to achieve occupancy goals.

Maven is our first AI agent:

MavenAI

Automating marketing across key channels like Google Business and Social.

Together, they move the industry from manual workflows to systems that understand, decide, and act.

What It's Like

The kind of team you actually want to be on.

We have a strict no assholes policy. Non-negotiable. But we go further than that, we're a low-ego, no-drama team that keeps things light while we build.

Our best-in-class employee retention is a result of our growth and pace, but we also believe building a sustainable company is a marathon, not a sprint. The vast majority of our team has been with us for 4+ years, and many have been with us for 8+ years.

We're a fully remote team spread across all U.S. timezones. That said, we like to see each other. We get together for an annual company-wide offsite, meet quarterly for team offsites, and find plenty of reasons to connect in person in between.

Competitive compensation. Flexible, paid time off. Medical, dental, and vision insurance. Short-term, long-term, and basic life insurance. Supplemental coverage, including pet insurance. 401(k) with employer match. Oh, and unlimited tokens.
